The app

Relocation guidance with sources attached.

RELO-MATE helps students understand what to prepare, what order to do it in, and which official notes the answer came from.

01

Verified guidance

Recommendations stay anchored to curated official source packs, making the checklist easier to trust and review.

02

Personal checklist

Nationality, destination, purpose, language, and arrival timing become a practical step-by-step relocation plan.

03

Progress tracker

Tasks, documents, deadlines, source notes, and completion status live in one focused dashboard.

04

Source chatbot

A lightweight RAG assistant answers questions only from the selected source pack and returns citations.
